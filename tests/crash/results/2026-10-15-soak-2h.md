## Notes

- **Holds**: +8.2% after two hours, below the +10% limit.
- The RSS still creeps after the warm-up, more slowly as the run goes on: +1.6 MB in the
  first hour after warm-up, +1.4 MB in the second. A run of several more hours may cross 10%.
- Each operation alone shows no growth: 400 to 600 repetitions of status, log, update
  (r1 <-> r2 and to new revisions of another user), commit, session open and `wc.db` open in one
  process each stay within 0.1 to 0.4 MB.
- The same soak with `MALLOC_ARENA_MAX=2` (10 minutes) starts at 18.8 MB instead of 32 MB and
  stays flat at 22.0 MB from minute 6 on. Most of the creep is glibc spreading allocations over
  more arenas as the tokio blocking pool starts and retires threads, not memory the engine keeps.
  Capping the arenas in the app (or a different allocator) would show as a lower and flatter
  RSS; nothing in this PR changes the allocator.
